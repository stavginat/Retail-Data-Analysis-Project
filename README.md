🛒 Retail Performance & Operational Audit: "Shefa Issachar" Supermarket
Data-driven optimization of retail operations, staffing, and customer growth using BigQuery & Looker Studio.

💡 Top Strategic Insights
1. 📉 Reducing Operational Overhead (Cost Saving)
My analysis revealed that the branch stays open during hours with zero to negligible customer traffic.

Action: Shorten operating hours during late-night shifts or specific mid-week slots.

Impact: Significant reduction in costs without affecting the customer experience or revenue.

2. ⚡ Unlocking Mid-Week Growth Potential
While weekends are the busiest, I identified a clear untapped segment of occasional customers during weekday evenings.

Action: Implement targeted marketing efforts specifically for evening hours on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

Impact: Converting occasional visitors into loyal, repeat customers to stabilize revenue and optimize store utilization during non-peak days.

3. 👥 Staffing Optimization
Detected 44 weekly "dead hours" where cashier presence was unnecessary, contrasted with peak times where the customer-to-cashier ratio exceeded 12:1.

Action: Re-align shift schedules based on the "Cashier Productivity" heatmap.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Methodology
SQL (BigQuery): Performed data cleaning, leveraged window functions for advanced data transformation, and executed complex joins to correlate dwell time with basket value.

Looker Studio: Built an interactive dashboard featuring Heatmaps, Trend Lines, and KPI scorecards.

Statistical Analysis: Conducted correlation tests (Pearson 0.997 for Sales/Volume) and linear regression for spend prediction.
